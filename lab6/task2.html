Lab Assignment Questions & Answers

Question: What is the effect of each margin value on the spacing between the elements?

  • Outer Div Margin (`margin: 25px auto`): Applies a 25px gap vertically above and below the outer container, pushing it away from other page elements, and centers it horizontally (`auto`) within the browser window.
  • Middle Div Margin (`margin-top: 20px; margin-bottom: 20px`): Creates 20px of vertical space above and below the middle container. This pushes it away from the inner boundaries of the outer div, controlling vertical flow spacing.
  • Inner Div Margin (`margin-left: 40px; margin-right: 40px`): Applies a 40px gap on both the left and right sides. This indents the innermost container horizontally inward, creating an offset visual hierarchy relative to its parent container.
